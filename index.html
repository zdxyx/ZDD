HUMBLE POSTAL BEGINNINGS

POSTAL is a 1997, shoot 'em up style game. The original game had pioneered as one of the first breakthrough games so raw and unfiltered that the game was even brought to court. However, the game creators, Vince Desi and Mike Jaret, both came from a much more PG video game history working under disney. The two made games under an entirely different studio name called XXX and worked on children's games Spy Vs. Spy and Jery XXX Bobby's world. After some work in the field of child-friendly games, the two decided they wanted to shift to more action packed games. They both came up with the studio RUNNING WITH SCISSORS and then began work on a game that had origins way past POSTAL. This game was Flesh & Wire which ultimately had to be dropped because the company supporting the game randomly withdrawled service. After this failure, RWS strived to create POSTAL.

POSTAL GAMEPLAY

POSTAL begins with POSTAL DUDE standing near the snowy terrain of his home. As you load into the game you start with numerous weapons and the task to eliminate all "hostiles". These hostiles will attempt to attack and kill POSTAL DUDE with weapons such as pistols and missle launchers. There are a total of 20 levels in POSTAL all ranging with different loading screen artwork and new terrain. In order to pass to the next level, you must decimate 80% of the population in total (this only concerns hostiles). You also have the option to kill citizens whilst defending yourself from hostiles and the ability to formally "execute" any hostile or citizen by pressing X. This isn't the smartest move to make however as executing one leaves you open to other hostile's attacks as the execute animation takes up about 3 seconds in gameplay which may seem miniscule, but every second in games such as this count for a mile. However while playing through all of these levels the fact that POSTAL DUDE even has the ability to kill regular citizens that have nothing to do with him while evading dangerous hostiles begs the questions: Is POSTAL DUDE really just protecting himself with intentions of staying alive, or is he just a blood-thirsty maniac who kills these hostiles not because they are a threat to him, but simply because claiming blood is a passion for him?

Well, i'll tell you one thing, nobody's truly after him, and the average player wouldn't know this fact until they played to the last few seocnds of the game.


ENDING

The original POSTAL ends with quite a disturbing ending. The ultimate "last" level of POSTAL involves POSTAL DUDE going to an elementary school and attempting to conduct a school shooting. However, DUDE finds the act simply too deranged and heart-breaking to which he collapses on the ground and the screen fades to white. The game then opens up to a fleshy prison cell with the voice of a doctor narrating THE DUDE'S predicament. You find out through his dialogue that POSTAL DUDE is actually mentally insanse and suffering from schizophrenia that caused him to believe that his neighbors and citizens of PARADISE, ARIZONA were after him. The doctor then ends with a chilling statemnt, saying that the modern pressures and circumstances of life got to him and finally made him crack. One might even say that the monotony of his life had finally made him go POSTAL. Which leads me to WHY the name is called POSTAL. It's based off of a phenomonon concerning POSTAL workers randomly going on streaks of violence and harming or killing their coworkers. This was caused by them being displaced after the Vietnam War and many soldiers who were forced back into regular modern life often retreated to the POSTAL service.

CORRELATION TO SOCIETY

POSTAL showcases the modern pressures and expectations of society in one of the most abrasive ways possible. Players go into POSTAL thinking it's a baseless game made only to urge its players into violece and chaos, but instead, players leave POSTAL with a new profound learning lession.

POST-POSTAL

After the release of POSTAL, 20 years later, POSTAL 2 released and was as intended by the creators to be designed as a “sociological experiment” that leaves violence entirely up to the player's discretion as none of the missions require any violent actions to be taken. The second game however, is more funny and satirical than the first game. POSTAL 2 allows the player to actually interact with the citizens of PARADISE, ARIZONA. However, because this is POSTAL, it can't be in a normal fashion. The player can interact with these citizens by either sticking the bird at them, waving a weapon infront of them, telling them to get down, or (my favorite) peeing on them. The player is able to piss on people for as long as they may until their piss bar reaches 0. Which of course, there is no worries as the piss bar steadily increases every time it reaches 0 which takes about 10 seconds to reach full capacity again. DUDE can also just walk around with his penis out which can allow for funny interactions such as women laughing at your size or potentially getting arrested. WHICH IS VERY POSSIBLE IN THIS GAME. In POSTAL 2 if you are committing illegal acts and a police officer is nearby the officer can pursue THE DUDE. You can bribe these officers to get out of trouble, drop your weapons and go with them to get arrested, or simply kill or run away from these officers. In this game you also find out that POSTAL DUDE has a wife beautifully named "Bitch". She is obese and stays in the trailer home that her and POSTAL DUDE own in Arizona. The game starts with a cutscene outlining the enviornment and aesthetic of PARADISE, ARIZONA and BITCH telling POSTAL DUDE to go out for some groceries and not to come in until his checklist is completed. The gameplay in the second game is much longer compared to its ancestor and allows the reader to play throughout an entire week of POSTAL DUDE's life. Each day in the week holds new prospects and challenges in PARADISE.


One of the tasks on Monday is to go to the RUNNING WITH SCISSORS headquarters to recieve your paycheck to which you find out that you were FIRED!!!! On the way up to the offices however, the player gets to encounter the RWS protesters that directly mirror and satirize the congressman and angry parents that were outraged at the release of POSTAL. They believed that POSTAL was a game only concerned with turning the younger generations into bloodthirsty maniacs who would go out into the streets and recreate the acts of POSTAL DUDE. RUNNING WITH SCISSORS saw this as "the dumbest thing they had ever seen" and decided it would be funny to add them into the game.
